# Image Generation Direction

When an image generator is available, produce image instructions systematically.

## Prompt anatomy

Describe:

1. Subject
2. Environment
3. Composition
4. Camera
5. Lighting
6. Materials
7. Brand colors
8. Graphic language
9. Mood
10. Output quality / aspect ratio

Example pattern:

> Commercial brand visual, [subject], in [environment], [composition], shot with [camera direction], [lighting], [materials], using [brand colors] and [graphic system], [mood], clean high-end art direction.

## Avoid vague-only prompts

Do not rely on:

- premium
- high-end
- beautiful
- designed
- stylish

Explain what creates the effect:

- restrained palette
- precise spacing
- matte material
- controlled highlights
- editorial typography
- shallow depth of field
- clean studio lighting

## Brand assets

If a supplied logo or package must remain accurate:

- preserve proportions
- do not invent label text
- avoid deforming product structure
- verify orientation and placement

## Human figures

Check:

- hands
- fingers
- arms
- joints
- face
- clothing overlap
- body-object interaction

## Product realism

Check:

- perspective
- packaging geometry
- cap/lid structure
- reflections
- material
- shadows
- scale
## Brand Asset Fidelity

Logos, wordmarks, packaging labels, and important typography must be treated as precision assets.

When a real logo file is supplied:

- preserve the original logo
- preserve exact proportions
- preserve orientation
- preserve approved colors
- do not redraw the logo from memory
- do not let an image generator reinterpret the logo

### Mockup Workflow

Prefer this workflow:

1. Generate or create the mockup environment.
2. Keep the branding surface clean or use a placeholder.
3. Apply the real logo and typography as a separate overlay when possible.
4. Check scale, perspective, and clear space.
5. Review the final result at 100% zoom.

### Text Accuracy

Do not rely on generative images for:

- small Chinese text
- addresses
- phone numbers
- product specifications
- legal copy
- ingredient lists
- exact slogans
- technical labels

These should be added with layout or vector tools when accuracy matters.

### Concept Render Label

If exact logo or typography cannot be preserved in the generated visual, treat the result as:

Concept Render

rather than a production-ready brand application.

Do not present approximate AI-generated branding as final production artwork.
Classify final applications as Concept, Presentation Ready, or Production Ready using `references/09-quality-control.md`.
# Brand Key Visual System

In Complete Brand Case Study mode, establish a recognizable Key Visual system before creating major application sections.

The Key Visual must be derived from the current brand strategy and visual identity.

Define:

- subject
- environment
- product or service context
- photography or illustration style
- lighting
- camera direction
- composition
- brand colors
- auxiliary graphics
- material and texture
- human presence when relevant
- emotional tone

## Brand Relevance Rule

Every Key Visual decision must support the current brand's:

- positioning
- target audience
- visual keywords
- product or service
- brand personality

Do not reuse the visual language of a previous brand simply because it produced an attractive result.

## Visual Consistency

The Key Visual should establish a recognizable visual atmosphere that can extend across:

- advertising
- packaging
- social media
- retail
- digital media
- lifestyle imagery

Applications should inherit the same core visual language.

Do not generate every application with a different:

- photography style
- lighting style
- color mood
- graphic language
- material direction

unless the brand system explicitly requires controlled variation.

## Key Visual Sequence

Depending on the brand, consider a sequence such as:

Brand atmosphere
↓
Product / service focus
↓
Lifestyle or usage scenario
↓
Detail
↓
Campaign visual
↓
Application adaptation

The exact sequence should adapt to the current project.

Do not force product photography, lifestyle photography, or human figures when they are irrelevant to the brand.
