# Quality Control

Review before final delivery.

## Brand fit

Score 0–10:

- clear positioning
- category relevance
- target-audience fit
- differentiation

## Logo integrity

Check:

- correct proportions
- sufficient contrast
- clear space
- approved colors
- no distortion

## Color

Check:

- primary color remains dominant
- support colors are controlled
- boards belong to one system
- digital text contrast is usable

## Typography

Check:

- hierarchy
- consistency
- readability
- limited type families
- appropriate line spacing

## Layout

Check:

- one focal point
- clear reading order
- consistent margins
- sufficient whitespace
- no accidental crowding

## Images

Check:

- image quality
- lighting consistency
- perspective
- product geometry
- anatomy
- label/logotype accuracy
- shadows/reflections

## Applications

Check whether one system genuinely extends across media instead of becoming separate styles.

## Overall scoring

Suggested 100-point framework:

- Brand Strategy: 10
- Visual Identity: 10
- Logo System: 10
- Color: 10
- Typography: 10
- Graphic System: 10
- Photography: 10
- Layout: 10
- Applications: 10
- Consistency & Commercial Quality: 10

Guidance:

- 90–100: ready for final presentation
- 80–89: refine weak areas
- below 80: revisit the system, not just surface styling
## Six-Board Consistency Test

Before final delivery, compare all VI boards side by side.

Check whether they clearly belong to the same brand.

Review:

- primary color
- secondary colors
- typography
- title placement
- grid
- margins
- logo scale
- auxiliary graphics
- photography
- lighting
- material
- visual density
- corner-radius system
- icon style

Ask:

> If the logo were removed from these boards, would they still look like the same brand?

If the answer is no, revise the inconsistent board.

## Anti-Template Check

Before final output, check whether the project looks like:

- generic AI cards
- generic presentation templates
- unrelated mockups
- random decorative geometry

If so, simplify the page and reinforce the brand-specific visual language.
